# NOAA AWC international SIGMET: documentation-only source gate

25 September 2026. **STOP before acquiring SIGMET bodies or using this source.**
The candidate is a previously received *international* SIGMET warning whose
hazard geometry overlaps a departure airport at a fixed UTC anchor. It is a
regional aviation hazard forecast, distinct in origin and spatial extent from
V3's airport METAR observations. This is a source hypothesis, not a measured
taxi-out benefit. The existing [TAF review](2026-09-25-taf-source-triage.md)
already identified AWC's 30-day limit and unresolved foreign-origin rights;
this review checks the international SIGMET-specific endpoint and fields.

| Gate | First-party evidence and decision |
| --- | --- |
| Correct product and geographic scope | The [AWC OpenAPI schema](https://aviationweather.gov/data/schema/openapi.yaml) distinguishes `/api/data/isigmet` (decoded international SIGMETs) from `/api/data/airsigmet` (domestic SIGMETs for the contiguous US, expressly excluding international-format messages). The [AWC product guide](https://aviationweather.gov/help/data/#sigmet) says non-CONUS international SIGMETs are defined within a flight information region (FIR) and include thunderstorms, turbulence, icing, volcanic ash and other hazards. The [API overview](https://aviationweather.gov/data/api/) advertises worldwide SIGMET coverage, but its `airsigmets.cache.*` files contain **current CONUS** warnings only. **Plausible Europe-facing product; the domestic endpoint/cache is not the ten-airport source.** Worldwide product scope does not prove any exact airport/month coverage. |
| Historical 2025 and January/July 2026 | [AWC's API](https://aviationweather.gov/data/api/) says its weather database reaches only the previous **30 days**; [Archive View](https://aviationweather.gov/tools/archive/) also offers up to 30 days and directs older NWS requests to NCEI AIRS. The [NCEI AIRS catalog](https://www.ncdc.noaa.gov/has/HAS.DsSelect) lists NOAAPort Text (NEW), 25 November 2000 to 24 September 2026, and SRRS Text Products, 26 April 2001 to 24 September 2026, at **dataset** level. These spans include all of 2025 and January/July 2026 in principle, but do not identify international SIGMET bulletins, four specific month cells, ten-airport FIRs, or a complete public retrieval manifest. **Fail documented exact historical support.** No order was placed. |
| Original issue, valid UTC and revision/as-of | The [international JSON schema](https://aviationweather.gov/data/schema/openapi.yaml) names `receiptTime` as "Time received by AWC" and gives integer `validTimeFrom`/`validTimeTo`, plus issuing-office `icaoId`, `firId`, `seriesId`, hazard and coordinates. It does **not** expose a separate structured original issue timestamp or an immutable version/replacement history for this endpoint; its GeoJSON properties omit `receiptTime`. AWC's 30-day retention cannot supply those receipt values for the historical cohorts, and the checked [NCEI catalog](https://www.ncdc.noaa.gov/has/HAS.DsSelect) advertises archive periods, not per-bulletin original delivery receipts or amendment/cancellation history. Valid-from time is not first-publication time. **Fail the campaign's historical as-of evidence gate.** This is a campaign provenance criterion, not an express organizer ban on retrospective SIGMETs. |
| Reuse and prize eligibility | [NWS terms](https://www.weather.gov/disclaimer) call NWS web-page information public domain unless noted and allow lawful use without misattribution or implied endorsement; they separately say third-party data may be used under provider licenses and direct users to the provider for reuse rights. The [AWC product guide](https://aviationweather.gov/help/data/#sigmet) says international SIGMETs are FIR-based, including those issued outside the US; its [schema](https://aviationweather.gov/data/schema/openapi.yaml) identifies each issuing office. The checked AWC/NCEI pages do not identify a dataset-specific open license or an original-producer grant for the *historical international* SIGMET archive relevant to Europe. The [organizer](https://prc-data-challenge-2026.netlify.app/eligibility.html#eligibility-for-prize) requires external datasets openly accessible/usable and documented and additional datasets openly available under an open-source license. **Hold prize use; NOAA's general public-domain text cannot settle foreign-origin archive rights or the organizer's literal license requirement.** This does not assert that every international SIGMET is restricted. |
| Airport join and novelty | The [challenge dictionary](https://prc-data-challenge-2026.netlify.app/data.html) names EDDF, EDDM, EGLL, EHAM, LEBL, LEMD, LFPG, LIRF, LTFM and LSZH, with departure `ADEP_mvt` and takeoff `MVT_TIME_UTC_mvt`. The [SIGMET schema](https://aviationweather.gov/data/schema/openapi.yaml) uses a **FIR/issuing office and warning coordinates**, not the departure's airport ICAO or flight ID. A conditional many-to-one join would pin a licensed airport-coordinate/FIR table, test polygon or line proximity at a frozen UTC anchor, require proof that each exact version was publicly disseminated before that anchor, and resolve updates/cancellations by version identity. AWC's internal `receiptTime` alone is insufficient. The anchor's retrospective takeoff clock is not itself proof of pre-off-block availability. V3 already has 18 METAR-derived `weather_T_*` fields including rain/thunder (see [local lightning review](2026-09-25-eumetsat-lightning.md)); regional severe-hazard geometry is a different input, but neither ten-airport support nor incremental variation has been tested. |

Reopen only with an explicit archive-specific reuse grant accepted for prize use,
a first-party manifest for the ten airports' relevant FIRs throughout 2025 and
January/July 2026, and publisher-backed first-publication evidence plus immutable
amendment and cancellation history for each version. Then freeze coordinate/FIR
mapping, geometry and altitude rules, public-dissemination cutoff and version precedence before any
input-only support audit. This review made 13 successful documentation requests
(under 2 MB total); it read no SIGMET bulletin, challenge row or target,
and produced no features, fit, score, prediction, submission or contact.
