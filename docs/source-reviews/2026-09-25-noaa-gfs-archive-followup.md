# NOAA GFS archive and reuse follow-up

25 September 2026. **HOLD: do not acquire forecast bodies or use this source in
the campaign.** This follows the [GFS source triage](2026-09-25-noaa-gfs-forecast-triage.md).
Only NOAA/NCEP product pages, NCEI catalog/object listings, HTTP HEAD metadata,
and published reuse terms were read. No `.grb2` or `.inv` body, airport field,
challenge record, label, fit, score, prediction, upload, or organizer contact was
used. The archive was queried during the September 25 review; HTTP response
`Date` headers from the host read September 26 UTC.

| Gate | First-party evidence | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Forecast calendar | [NCEI's GFS page](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/weather-climate-models/global-forecast) lists Grid 004 (0.5 degree) forecasts from 10 October 2006 to present, approximately two years online, four 00/06/12/18 UTC cycles daily, with three-hourly +000 through +192 hours. Its distinct cloud feed is a *trailing 30-day* 0.25/0.5-degree window. A filename's cycle plus lead is a forecast **valid** time, not a publication time: e.g. `20260701_0000_003` represents a July 1 00 UTC run nominally valid 03 UTC. | **Product-level PASS**, subject to exact object/field coverage. The 30-day NODD bucket cannot furnish these old months. |
| January/July 2025 objects | The NCEI [THREDDS Grid-4 forecast index](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/thredds/catalog/model-gfs-004-files/catalog.xml) stops at `202508` and includes [January 2025](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/thredds/catalog/model-gfs-004-files/202501/catalog.xml) (31 day catalogs) and [July 2025](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/thredds/catalog/model-gfs-004-files/202507/catalog.xml) (**30** day catalogs; `20250709` absent, and [the direct day catalog](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/thredds/catalog/model-gfs-004-files/202507/20250709/catalog.xml) returned 404). Sample [January 1](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/thredds/catalog/model-gfs-004-files/202501/20250101/catalog.xml) and [July 1](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/thredds/catalog/model-gfs-004-files/202507/20250701/catalog.xml) catalogs each list 12 files: 00/06/12/18 cycles, but only +000/+003/+006 leads on those days. The sample files are named `gfs_3_...` **inside a Grid-4 catalog**, an unresolved product/grid identity mismatch. [January +003](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/thredds/fileServer/model-gfs-004-files/202501/20250101/gfs_3_20250101_0000_003.grb2) and [July +003](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/thredds/fileServer/model-gfs-004-files/202507/20250701/gfs_3_20250701_0000_003.grb2) returned HEAD 200 with last-modified August 21, **2026** (14:57:15 and 15:07:23 UTC), not an original-2025 receipt. The current [NCEI Grid-004 object listing](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/oa/prod-model?list-type=2&prefix=global-forecast-system/access/grid-004-0.5-degree/forecast/&delimiter=%2F&max-keys=100) skips all 2025 month prefixes (202311 then 202512); a targeted [202501 listing](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/oa/prod-model?list-type=2&prefix=global-forecast-system/access/grid-004-0.5-degree/forecast/202501/&delimiter=%2F&max-keys=10) has zero keys. | **HOLD** exact 2025 Grid-004 support. Some current THREDDS forecast objects exist, but their grid identity, lead coverage, missing July 9, and object-store disparity preclude a complete four-cell claim. |

The [20260101 `gfs_4` +003 object](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/oa/prod-model?list-type=2&prefix=global-forecast-system/access/grid-004-0.5-degree/forecast/202601/20260101/gfs_4_20260101_0000_003.grb2&max-keys=2) is 159,625,962 bytes, ETag `fe1f0062c957e345a99e7ab310c0563a`, current last-modified **2026-08-21 16:42:37.692 UTC**. | Original publication and immutable versions | NCEI's [20260101 +003 version listing](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/oa/prod-model?versions&prefix=global-forecast-system/access/grid-004-0.5-degree/forecast/202601/20260101/gfs_4_20260101_0000_003.grb2&max-keys=5) and [20260701 +003 version listing](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/oa/prod-model?versions&prefix=global-forecast-system/access/grid-004-0.5-degree/forecast/202607/20260701/gfs_4_20260701_0000_003.grb2&max-keys=5) each return `VersionId` **`null`** for the latest GRIB and its `.inv` sidecar, with no earlier GRIB version in these exact-key responses. The 2026 sample `LastModified` timestamps occur months or days after the encoded cycle; 2025 THREDDS sample dates occur over a year later. ETags identify current bytes but are neither original publication receipts nor immutable version IDs. A HEAD request of [July 2026 +003](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/oa/prod-model/global-forecast-system/access/grid-004-0.5-degree/forecast/202607/20260701/gfs_4_20260701_0000_003.grb2) returned the same current last-modified/ETag and no version ID or first-publication timestamp. | **FAIL the historical as-of gate.** A cycle is model initialization, valid time is forecast target, and current modification is current-object metadata. None proves those exact bytes were publicly available before a departure or rules out replacement. `null` is not proof that no prior copy ever existed elsewhere. |
| Prize reuse | The [NOAA-managed GFS NODD listing](https://registry.opendata.aws/noaa-gfs-bdp-pds/) labels its license: data disseminated through NODD are open to the public and "can be used as desired"; NOAA requests attribution for unaltered data and bars implying endorsement or representing modified data as original. [NWS's data terms](https://www.weather.gov/disclaimer) say NWS web-page information is public domain unless otherwise noted, subject to lawful-use and representation safeguards, but they also warn about third-party data. The NCEI [Grid-4 THREDDS catalog](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/thredds/catalog/model-gfs-004-files/catalog.xml) says electronic downloads are free, with fees for certification or physical media; it does **not** state a named open-source data license. The [organizer's rule](https://prc-data-challenge-2026.netlify.app/eligibility.html#eligibility-for-prize) requires used external datasets to be openly accessible/usable and documented, and all additional datasets to be openly available **under an open source license**. | **HOLD literal prize compatibility.** NODD's reuse grant applies to NODD distribution, while the campaign's older objects are in the NCEI archive; "free download" is not a license. The public-domain/NOAA reuse language may be substantively open, but no archive-specific named grant or organizer acceptance of that wording was established. This does not imply NOAA prohibits reuse. |
| Airport support and access | NCEI's global grid, day/cycle/lead keys, THREDDS fileServer and object-store HTTP access could support a deterministic airport-to-grid-cell/time join. The object index exposes `.inv` sidecar **names and sizes** (e.g. [20260101 +003 `.inv`](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/oa/prod-model?list-type=2&prefix=global-forecast-system/access/grid-004-0.5-degree/forecast/202601/20260101/gfs_4_20260101_0000_003.grb2.inv&max-keys=1)), but no field contents were read. The [organizer's dictionary](https://prc-data-challenge-2026.netlify.app/data.html) provides departure ICAO and UTC movement time, not a licensed airport coordinate table. | **Plausible access, unproven support.** Freeze a reusable ten-ICAO coordinate source, projection/cell and tie rules, a predeparture publication cutoff, lead/valid-time convention, field identity and missingness policy. Then audit all 40 airport-month cells and every eligible cycle/lead by metadata before any values. |

**Decisive next step:** obtain publisher-backed first-publication and immutable
revision receipts for the *exact* archive objects across January/July 2025/2026,
reconcile `gfs_3` versus Grid-004 and the missing July 9, and establish an
archive-specific reuse grant accepted under the organizer's open-source-license
wording. If those cannot be established, stop the GFS arm; do not substitute a
conservative lag or current object's `LastModified` for historical availability.
Only after these gates pass should an input-only airport/field support audit be
considered. No RMSE improvement is inferred from archive availability.
